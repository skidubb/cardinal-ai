# CFO Skill: Financial Analysis for Professional Services

## When to Use
Invoke this skill when analyzing project profitability, reviewing financial performance, or conducting financial due diligence for a consulting/agency business.

## Analysis Framework

### 1. Revenue Analysis
- **Net Revenue Calculation**: Gross revenue minus pass-through costs (subcontractors, expenses)
- **Revenue Quality**: Recurring vs. project vs. T&M mix
- **Revenue Concentration**: Top client dependency risk
- **Growth Analysis**: Organic vs. acquired, by service line

### 2. Profitability Cascade
```
Gross Revenue
- Pass-through costs
= Net Revenue (100%)
- Direct labor costs (typically 40-55%)
= Gross Profit (typically 45-60%)
- Practice overhead (management, tools, training)
= Contribution Margin (typically 35-50%)
- Firm overhead (admin, facilities, marketing)
= Operating Profit (EBITDA) (typically 10-25%)
```

### 3. Project-Level Analysis
For each project, assess:
- **Quoted vs. Actual Margin**: Identify margin erosion patterns
- **Rate Realization**: Standard rate ÷ Actual billed rate
- **Scope Creep Impact**: Unbilled hours as % of total
- **Client Profitability Tier**: A/B/C clients by margin

### 4. Key Ratios
| Ratio | Healthy Range | Warning Signs |
|-------|--------------|---------------|
| Gross Margin | 50-65% | <45% indicates pricing or utilization issues |
| EBITDA Margin | 15-25% | <10% indicates structural cost problems |
| Revenue/FTE | $200-400K | <$150K indicates low rates or utilization |
| DSO | 30-45 days | >60 days indicates collection issues |

### 5. Benchmarking Sources
- Service Performance Insight (SPI) Research
- Deltek Clarity Architecture & Engineering Study
- Hinge Research Institute High Growth Study
- RAIN Group benchmarks
