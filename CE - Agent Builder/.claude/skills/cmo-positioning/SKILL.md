# CMO Skill: Brand Positioning for Professional Services

## When to Use
Invoke this skill when developing or refining brand positioning, differentiating from competitors, or defining target market focus.

## Positioning Framework

### 1. Positioning Canvas

```markdown
## Positioning Canvas

### Target Client Profile
**Industry**: [Specific industries or verticals]
**Company Size**: [Revenue range, employee count]
**Buyer Role**: [Title, function, decision authority]
**Situation**: [What's happening that creates need]

### Problem/Need Statement
[The specific, urgent problem your target clients face]
- Pain points: [What hurts]
- Consequences of inaction: [What happens if they don't solve it]
- Current alternatives: [How they're trying to solve it now]

### Category Definition
"We are a [type of firm] that specializes in [focus area]"
- Primary category: [How you want to be classified]
- Adjacent categories: [Related spaces you touch]

### Key Benefit
The ONE primary benefit clients get from working with you:
[Benefit statement - focused, specific, valuable]

### Proof Points
Evidence that you deliver this benefit:
1. [Case study / result]
2. [Client testimonial]
3. [Proprietary methodology]
4. [Industry recognition]

### Differentiation
Why you, not them:
| vs. Big Consultancies | vs. Boutiques | vs. In-house |
|----------------------|---------------|--------------|
| [Your advantage] | [Your advantage] | [Your advantage] |

### Brand Character
How you show up:
- Voice: [Formal/casual, expert/approachable]
- Personality: [Traits that define your brand]
- Visual: [Design direction]
```

### 2. Positioning Statement Formula

**For** [target client with specific need]
**Who** [key challenge or opportunity]
**Our firm** is a [category]
**That** [key benefit/outcome]
**Unlike** [primary alternative]
**We** [key differentiator]

### 3. Differentiation Strategies

| Strategy | Description | Works When |
|----------|-------------|------------|
| **Specialization** | Deep expertise in narrow area | Market is large enough, expertise is rare |
| **Methodology** | Proprietary approach/framework | Process creates better outcomes |
| **Outcomes** | Guarantee or focus on results | You can quantify and deliver |
| **Experience** | Specific industry/function expertise | Credibility from domain knowledge |
| **Service Model** | Different delivery approach | You can serve better/faster/cheaper |
| **Values** | Purpose-driven positioning | Clients share values |

### 4. Competitive Analysis Matrix

| Factor | Weight | You | Competitor A | Competitor B |
|--------|--------|-----|--------------|--------------|
| Expertise depth | 25% | 4 | 5 | 3 |
| Industry focus | 20% | 5 | 3 | 4 |
| Pricing | 15% | 3 | 2 | 4 |
| Team quality | 20% | 4 | 5 | 3 |
| Service delivery | 20% | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| **Weighted Score** | 100% | X.X | X.X | X.X |
