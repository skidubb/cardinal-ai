# CFO Skill: Cash Flow Management for Professional Services

## When to Use
Invoke this skill when forecasting cash flow, analyzing working capital, or addressing cash management challenges in a consulting/agency business.

## Cash Flow Framework

### 1. Cash Conversion Cycle
```
Cash Cycle = Sales Cycle + WIP Days + DSO - DPO

Where:
- Sales Cycle: Average days from opportunity to signed contract
- WIP Days: Average days work is performed before invoicing
- DSO: Days Sales Outstanding (invoice to payment)
- DPO: Days Payables Outstanding (when you pay suppliers)
```

**Typical Professional Services Cash Cycle: 45-90 days**

### 2. Working Capital Components

#### Accounts Receivable Analysis
- **Current (0-30 days)**: Target >70% of AR
- **31-60 days**: Should be <20%
- **61-90 days**: Requires active collection
- **90+ days**: Bad debt risk, reserve consideration

#### Work-in-Progress (WIP)
- **Healthy WIP**: <30 days of revenue equivalent
- **Warning Signs**: Growing WIP, old WIP, disputed WIP
- **Best Practice**: Invoice at least monthly, negotiate retainers

### 4. Cash Flow Improvement Levers

**Accelerate Inflows:**
- Retainer/prepayment arrangements
- Progress billing (milestone-based)
- Faster invoicing (weekly vs. monthly)
- Online payment options
- Collection process improvement

**Optimize Outflows:**
- Payroll timing optimization
- Vendor payment term negotiation
- Credit card float utilization
- Equipment leasing vs. purchasing

### 5. Cash Reserve Guidelines
- **Minimum**: 2 months operating expenses
- **Healthy**: 3-4 months operating expenses
- **Growth-ready**: 6+ months for acquisition/expansion
